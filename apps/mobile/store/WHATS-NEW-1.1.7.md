# What's New — 1.1.7 (build 54)

Previous release: **1.1.6 = build 53**. 1.1.7 carries the app fixes merged
since then; see `docs/release-1.1.7.md`.

```
No more blank screens: if your dashboard was cleared while ChairBack sat in the background, it now reloads by itself when you come back. Loading and "Couldn't load" screens are easier to read, with a Retry that works.

Add your appointment to Apple Wallet right from its details.

See every rewards tier, what it takes and what you get, on the Rewards tab.

Shops with a dark page now get a matching dark bar at the top when you book.
```
